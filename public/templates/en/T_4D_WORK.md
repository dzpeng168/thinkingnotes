## 🔵 Delegate it

> **Work that can be assigned or delegated to others for you.**
>
> By delegating and assigning, you free up more time for what matters most.
>
> *e.g. Ask a subordinate to attend a non-critical forum; ask your boss for help with an approval outside your scope.*

## 🟢 Do it now

> **Things you can finish in 2 minutes.**
>
> Anything that takes two or three minutes — do it immediately. Prevent these small items from occupying mental bandwidth.
>
> *e.g. Send a spreadsheet to Finance; send a confirmation email to a vendor; jot down a note for a newly added friend.*

## 🟡 Defer it

> **Things you can't delegate and can't finish in 2 minutes.**
>
> Work that can't be done immediately, needs a large block of time, and can't be delegated — write it down and put it on the to-do list. Writing it down lets you temporarily forget about it so your brain can focus on the current task; pick new items from the list once the current work is done.

## 🔴 Delete it

> **Things you can't delegate, can't finish in 2 minutes, and don't need to do.**
>
> Abandon and delete these decisively. Don't let trivia occupy your mind — clear it out.
>
> *e.g. An irrelevant meeting. An unimportant email.*
