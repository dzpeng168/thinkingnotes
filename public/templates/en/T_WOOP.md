## ⚠️ Usage Notes

1. **Wish must follow SMART principles**：Specific, measurable, achievable, observable, and time-bound.
2. **Start with small goals**：When first using the WOOP model, start with small goals to get early wins and build confidence.
3. **One wish per WOOP**：Don't mix multiple wishes in one sheet. Analyze each wish separately.
4. **Be honest about obstacles**：Don't avoid your true inner obstacles. The more real the obstacle, the more effective the plan.
5. **Be specific with actions**：The action after "then" must be immediately executable. Avoid vague wording.
