**Opening line reference**：

**Checklist**：

- [ ] Amplified appropriately, not overstated
- [ ] Directly related to the other party's interests

## 💡 Usage Tips

**Order matters**：
```
Risk (loss aversion) → Interest (gain vs. loss) → Difference (unique appeal) → Effect (honest credibility)
```

**Use cases**：
- Presenting proposals to superiors
- Selling products/services to clients
- Cross-department coordination for resources
- Convincing colleagues to accept your idea

**Key principles**：
1. **Loss before gain** — Lead with risk, follow with benefit. Use loss-aversion psychology.
2. **Sharp contrast** — Risk and benefit should form a strong contrast.
3. **Emphasize uniqueness** — What others don't have I offer; what others have I do better.
4. **Modest self-criticism** — Volunteer small shortcomings to build trust.
5. **Hand back the decision** — Persuasion isn't coercion; give the other party choice.
