## 💡 Usage Tips

**Use cases**：
- Reporting to superiors
- Speaking up in meetings
- Writing emails / copy
- Answering interview questions
- Persuading others

**Notes**：
1. **Point must be clear** — No ambiguity. State it in one sentence.
2. **Reasons must be strong** — 2 to 3 of your most persuasive reasons are enough.
3. **Examples must be specific** — Details, data, comparisons make it more convincing.
4. **Reiteration must be concise** — Use different wording to re-emphasize; don't just repeat verbatim.
5. **Leave space for the other party** — After expressing your point, hand the decision back to them.
