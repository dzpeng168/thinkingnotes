# Empathy Map

> **Target Persona**: Product Manager in Tech · Alex (28 years old, 3 years of experience)  
> **Date**: 2026-08-30  
> **Analyst**: User Research Team  
> **Context**: We are developing a productivity tool for product managers, hoping to deeply understand the real needs and pain points of target users through an empathy map

## 📝 Insight Summary

**Core insights**:
1. The core pain point of product managers is not "not enough features" but rather "anxiety" and "lack of sense of value" — a tool should not only boost efficiency but also give them security and a sense of achievement
2. On the surface they say they need "better requirement management tools," but deep down they crave "recognition, growth, and freedom from anxiety"
3. They appear confident and professional externally but constantly doubt themselves internally — this is an important emotional entry point

**User's most fundamental need**:
> A tool that lets product managers "increase efficiency AND reduce anxiety" — not just a functional tool, but a companion for career growth

**Actionable directions**:
- [ ] Add "achievement" elements to product design (e.g., task completion visualization, growth trajectory tracking)
- [ ] Shift marketing copy from "productivity tool" toward "product manager's growth partner"
- [ ] Add "career development" related content (not just tools, but also community and growth paths)
