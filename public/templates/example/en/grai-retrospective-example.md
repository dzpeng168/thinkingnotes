## G — Goal Review

### Purpose Goal
> Why are we doing this? What is the underlying purpose and meaning?

Achieve three objectives through the 618 mid-year sale:
1. Drive performance, hit 40% of H1 GMV target
2. Acquire new users through promotional discounts
3. Brand exposure, increase industry visibility

### Task Goal (Quantified)
> What specific measurable results are to be achieved?

| Metric | Target | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| GMV | 500 | $10K (5M) |
| New users | 20 | K users |
| Conversion rate | 8 | % |
| AOV | 200 | $ |

### Action Goal (Method)
> What methods and paths are planned to achieve the goals?

- Launch preparation 1 month early, establish cross-department task force
- 3 rounds of pre-heat: Blog post → Community referral → Splash-screen ads
- Main venue + 5 themed sub-venues
- First attempt at live-stream commerce: 2 sessions daily, invite 3 top influencers
- Partner with 5 brand collaborators for joint promotion

### Goal Completion

| Goal type | Target | Actual | Completion | Score |

### Highlights (1-3)
1. **Live-stream exceeded expectations**: Contributed 30% of GMV — the biggest pleasant surprise of this campaign, validated the feasibility of live-stream e-commerce
2. **High existing user repurchase rate**: Reached 35%, indicating daily user operations are working and user stickiness is improving
3. **Smooth cross-department collaboration**: No chaotic situations like last year; advance rehearsal and clear division of labor worked

### Shortcomings (1-3)
1. **Server downtime**: Server crashed for 20 minutes at 10:00 on day one; conservative estimate $20K GMV loss, also damaged user experience
2. **Best-sellers out of stock**: TOP 3 products sold out 2 days early; traffic came later but nothing to sell — estimated loss $15K
3. **Customer support lagged**: User wait time went from 30 seconds usual to 5 minutes; complaints tripled, damaging brand reputation

| Reason | Type | Impact |
|---|---|---|

| Inventory forecast only referenced historical data, no new variable model | Subjective (methodology issue) | 🟡 Medium |
| Insufficient support staffing; misjudged campaign scale | Subjective (inaccurate estimate) | 🟡 Medium |

### 5 Whys Deep Dive (Optional)

```
Problem: Why did the server crash?
  → Because traffic peak exceeded server capacity
  → Why did traffic exceed?
    → Because live-stream brought far more traffic than expected
    → Why wasn't live-stream traffic anticipated?
      → Because there was no live-stream last year, no historical data to reference
      → Why not make a more conservative estimate?
        → Because of cost concerns, wanted "just enough" — and it backfired
        → Root cause: Insufficient traffic risk assessment for new channels; capacity strategy too conservative. Should be "better to over-prepare than under-prepare"
```

## I — Insight

### Reusable Methodology / SOP

**Methods that can be reused next time**:
1. Big sale full-link stress testing SOP — Start 2 weeks early, at least 3 rounds
2. Live-stream product selection and operations SOP — Selection criteria, host coordination, traffic handling
3. Cross-department collaboration mechanism — Weekly meeting + daily standup + real-time chat group + clear RACI

**Processes to formalize**:
- Step 1: 1 month before sale, complete all plan reviews and resource confirmation
- Step 2: 2 weeks before sale, begin full-link stress testing and simulation rehearsal
- Step 3: 1 week before sale, all staff on standby, do final checks
- Step 4: During sale, daily retrospective, quickly adjust strategy
- Step 5: Within 1 week after sale, complete full retrospective document

### Q&A Compilation

| Q Question | A Answer | Applicable scenario |
|---|---|---|
| How to estimate server capacity? | Historical peak × 2, × 1.5 more if new channels exist | All large-scale promotions |
| How much inventory to stock? | 60% in first batch, replenish based on real-time data | E-commerce campaigns |
| How to staff customer support? | 3-5x usual, prepare auto-replies | All large-scale events |
| How to handle live-stream traffic? | Prepare landing pages in advance, ensure server can handle | Live-stream commerce scenarios |
