# Test: My Trust Level with My Direct Manager

> **Trust = (Credibility + Reliability + Intimacy) / Self-orientation**
>
> From *The Trusted Advisor*. A self-assessment of my working relationship with my manager after one year, checked item by item to see where the trust really stands.

**Assessed relationship**: Direct manager · Sam (working together for 1 year 2 months)

---

## 🎯 Credibility (Do my words hold up?)

**Positive**

- [x] Proven high-quality results (Q2 growth review cited by the director)
- [x] Constructive feedback
- [ ] Prestigious certificates / reputation
- [ ] Sees the essence of complex issues
- [ ] Solves complex problems

**Negative**

- [x] Insufficient expertise (real gap after moving into the growth domain)
- [ ] Lack of independent thinking
- [ ] Pretending to know
- [x] Only criticizing without suggestions (occasionally only nitpicking in design reviews)

## ⏰ Reliability (Do my actions hold up?)

**Positive**

- [x] Keeps promises
- [x] Organized work
- [ ] Punctual (late to weekly sync twice…)
- [x] Doesn't over-promise
- [x] Calm under pressure

**Negative**

- [x] Misses deadlines (analytics dashboard slipped two weeks in a row)
- [ ] Quality compromises
- [x] Prioritizes urgent-over-important (constantly interrupted by firefighting)
- [ ] Repeating mistakes

## 💞 Intimacy (How close are we?)

**Positive**

- [x] Frequent contact (weekly 1:1)
- [x] Knows personal details (knows he cares most about data rigor)
- [ ] Shared interests / values
- [x] Proactive attention
- [ ] Shared hardships (never weathered a major incident together)

**Negative**

- [x] No contact outside work (zero personal connection)
- [ ] Don't know each other
- [x] No deep communication (career planning never came up)
- [ ] Being unknown to the other

## 🪞 Self-orientation (the denominator — the lower the better)

**Behaviors that lower S**

- [x] Faces problems proactively
- [x] Shares credit
- [ ] Seeks solutions before excuses (last slip, I led with the reasons)
- [ ] Cooperative mindset

**Behaviors that raise S (watch out)**

- [ ] Avoiding problems
- [x] Making excuses (yes, see above)
- [ ] Taking credit / blaming others
- [ ] Self-centered

---

## 📊 Conclusion & Actions

- **Current trust level**: Above medium. The C and R base is solid, but I and S drag the overall level down — that's the intuition of the equation: however large the numerator, a large denominator dilutes trust all the same.
- **Weakest element**: I (Intimacy). Our 1:1s stay strictly business; we've never talked about growth aspirations. On S, I need to break the "explain first, solve later" habit.
- **Next actions**:
  1. In next week's 1:1, proactively ask for one piece of career advice — open one deep conversation;
  2. Switch delivery rhythm to "halfway sync on Wednesday" — transparency cures slipping;
  3. In the next review, offer an alternative before pointing out problems.
