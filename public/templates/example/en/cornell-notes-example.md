## 📌 Summaries
> Refine core ideas in your own words; review, summarize, and organize questions

1. Growth hacking is not a "black magic trick" but a data-driven scientific methodology — the core is rapid experimentation and rapid iteration
2. The AARRR funnel is a classic framework for analyzing the user lifecycle, but different products emphasize different things: tool products focus on activation and retention, e-commerce focuses on revenue and conversion
3. The North Star Metric is extremely important — it acts like a beacon guiding the entire team in the same direction. Choose correctly, and you get twice the result with half the effort; choose wrong, and you go astray
4. Growth experiments are the core daily work of growth. The ICE scoring method is a simple and practical prioritization tool

**Questions**:
- What is the relationship between the North Star Metric and OKRs?
- Can traditional industries apply growth hacking methods?
- What sample size is needed for growth experiments to be statistically significant?
