## 🎯 S — Specific

> Goals must be clear and unambiguous. Answer: Who? Doing what? Where?

| Dimension | Content |
|---|---|
| **Goal Description** | In Q4 (October-December), grow product Monthly Active Users (MAU) from 500K to 800K, a 60% increase |
| **People Involved** | Growth team of 5 (lead), Product team of 3 (support feature optimization), Operations team of 4 (activities and content), Marketing team of 2 (ad spend and branding) |
| **Specific Items** | 1. Optimize new user registration conversion funnel<br>2. Launch existing-user referral program<br>3. Add 3 new acquisition channels<br>4. Improve user day-1 retention rate |
| **Locations/Scenarios** | Online product (App + Mini-program), online ad channels (TikTok/Xiaohongshu/WeChat Ads), offline partner channels (2) |

**One-sentence goal statement**:
> Within the three months of Q4 2026, grow product MAU from 500K to 800K by optimizing the conversion funnel, running a referral program, and multi-channel user acquisition.

**Why does this matter?**
- The company's core strategy this year is "user scale first," and MAU is the single most important North Star metric
- Year-end fundraising needs user data support; 800K MAU is the threshold for the next round of funding
- Once scale is achieved, commercialization has much greater upside potential
